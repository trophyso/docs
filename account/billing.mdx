## Usage-based billing {#usage-based-billing}

Trophy charges by [monthly active users](#monthly-active-users-maus) (MAUs), so you never pay for churn.

## Monthly active users (MAUs) {#monthly-active-users-maus}

Trophy counts how many unique users you've told Trophy about each month. As long as a user sends at least one [metric event](/data-sources/events) in a given month, they count as an active user.

Users who aren't active in later months aren't charged. If a user signs up for your product in a given month but doesn't return, you only pay for that user once and never again.

## Plans {#plans}

Trophy has three plans, <PlanBadge plan="pro" />, <PlanBadge plan="business" /> and <PlanBadge plan="enterprise" />.

### Pro plan {#pro-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="pro" /> plan is for teams who want to explore AI customer engagement. It includes:

- [AI lifecycle agents](/agentic-messaging/overview)
- In-app personalization: [milestones](/features/milestones), [streaks](/features/streaks), [points](/features/points), [leaderboards](/features/leaderboards) and [rewards](/features/rewards)
- A dedicated Slack channel

### Business plan {#business-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="business" /> plan is for teams with additional security and support needs. It includes everything in <PlanBadge plan="pro" />, plus:

- [Single sign-on](/account/security#single-sign-on-sso)
- [Multi-factor authentication](/account/security#multi-factor-authentication-mfa)
- [Role-based access control](/account/security#role-based-access-control-rbac)

### Enterprise plan {#enterprise-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="enterprise" /> plan is for high-volume applications with bespoke needs. It includes everything in <PlanBadge plan="business" />, plus:

- Data residency control
- Custom uptime SLA
- SOC 2 / ISO reports

[Contact sales](https://trophy.so/contact-sales?utm_source=docs) to discuss pricing.
