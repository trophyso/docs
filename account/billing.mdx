## Usage-based Billing {#usage-based-billing}

Trophy follows a usage-based pricing model where customers only pay for the units of usage they consume. For Trophy, a unit of usage corresponds to a single [Monthly Active User](#monthly-active-users-maus) (MAU).

<Info>
  See our [pricing page](https://trophy.so/pricing) to get an estimate of your
  costs based on your expected usage.
</Info>

## Monthly Active Users (MAUs) {#monthly-active-users-maus}

Trophy defines an MAU as a single user that sends at least one [metric event](/features/events) to Trophy in a given month.

Bear in mind you never pay for churned users. If a user signs up for your product in a given month but doesn't return, you only pay for that user once and never again.

## Plans {#plans}

Trophy has three plans, <PlanBadge plan="pro" />, <PlanBadge plan="business" /> and <PlanBadge plan="enterprise" />.

### Pro Plan {#pro-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="pro" /> plan is for teams running Trophy in production. It includes all of Trophy's core gamification features: achievements, streaks, points, leaderboards, emails and push notifications.

### Business Plan {#business-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="business" /> plan is for teams that need additional security, access controls and support, including single sign-on, role-based access control, data sync and a dedicated Slack channel.

### Enterprise Plan {#enterprise-plan}

The <PlanBadge plan="enterprise" /> plan is for larger organizations with custom requirements such as multi-tenancy, data residency, compliance reports and custom contracts. [Get in touch](mailto:support@trophy.so) to discuss pricing.

### Plan Allowances {#plan-allowances}

Here's a comparison of the allowances of each plan:

| Item                        | <PlanBadge plan="pro" /> | <PlanBadge plan="business" /> | <PlanBadge plan="enterprise" /> |
| --------------------------- | -------- | -------- | ---------- |
| Base price                  | `$299`   | `$599`   | Custom     |
| Included MAUs               | `10,000` | `10,000` | Custom     |
| Included Emails             | `20,000` | `20,000` | Custom     |
| Included Push Notifications | `20,000` | `20,000` | Custom     |

### Overages {#overages}

Overages are charged on the <PlanBadge plan="pro" /> and <PlanBadge plan="business" /> plans above the included [allowances](#plan-allowances) at the following rates:

| Item                     | <PlanBadge plan="pro" /> | <PlanBadge plan="business" /> |
| ------------------------ | -------- | -------- |
| 1 MAU                    | `$0.015` | `$0.015` |
| 1,000 Emails             | `$2.00`  | `$2.00`  |
| 1,000 Push Notifications | `$1.00`  | `$1.00`  |

Volume discounts are available as part of [custom contracts](#custom-contracts).
