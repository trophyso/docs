Trophy provides a number of security features to help you control who can access your organization and how they sign in. For details on how Trophy protects your data, visit our [Trust Center](https://trust.trophy.so).

## Single Sign-On (SSO) {#single-sign-on-sso}

Single sign-on lets your team members sign in to Trophy using your organization's identity provider, so access to Trophy is managed alongside the rest of your tools.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) {#multi-factor-authentication-mfa}

Multi-factor authentication adds an extra verification step when team members sign in, protecting your account if a password is compromised.

## Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) {#role-based-access-control-rbac}

Role-based access control lets you manage what each team member can do in your Trophy organization based on their role. To learn about the default roles available to every organization, see [Members](/account/members#manage-user-roles).

## Audit Logs {#audit-logs}

Audit logs give you a record of activity in your Trophy organization. Business plan accounts retain audit logs for 30 days.
