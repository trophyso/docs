Objectives tell agents what success looks like for your product. For each user, agents send the messages most likely to move your objectives, and Trophy reports the impact of agentic messaging on each one.

Agents aren't working through a send schedule. Every decision they make, including the decision not to send, is made in service of your objectives.

## Types of objectives {#types-of-objectives}

Agentic messaging supports four objectives, covering every stage of the user lifecycle:

- **Activation** helps new users reach the moments that make your app valuable, like finishing onboarding or completing a first lesson.
- **Engagement** gets active users to do more of what matters in your app, like logging workouts or solving puzzles.
- **Conversion** moves users toward the actions that drive revenue, like starting a trial, upgrading or making a purchase.
- **Retention** keeps users coming back to your app. This is the default: if you don't set any objectives, agentic messaging works towards retention.

## Weighting objectives {#weighting-objectives}

You can combine objectives and weight the specific results that matter most to your business, measured with your [metrics](/data-sources/metrics). For example, an upgrade might count for more than a completed lesson.

The weights tell agents what each result is worth, so they chase the actions that matter most. Agents also adjust for each user: each agent works toward the objective that fits where its user is now, whether that's a new user who hasn't activated yet or a habitual user who's starting to slip.

## Viewing objectives {#viewing-objectives}

Your current objectives and their shares are listed on the [Objectives](https://app.trophy.so/agentic-messaging/objectives) page under **Agentic messaging** in the Trophy dashboard.

<Note>
  Objectives are set with the Trophy team during
  [rollout](/agentic-messaging/rollout). To change your objectives, reach out to
  your Trophy contact or [email us](mailto:support@trophy.so).
</Note>

## Measuring objectives {#measuring-objectives}

Each objective gets its own row in the impact table on the agentic messaging [Analytics](/agentic-messaging/measuring-impact) page. Agents are compared against a holdout group, so you see the lift on each objective rather than just opens and clicks.
