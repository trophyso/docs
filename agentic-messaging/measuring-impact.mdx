Trophy measures agentic messaging against a holdout group, so you see the incremental lift on your [objectives](/agentic-messaging/objectives), not just opens and clicks.

You'll find everything on this page on the [Analytics](https://app.trophy.so/agentic-messaging) page under **Agentic messaging** in the Trophy dashboard.

## Cohorts {#cohorts}

Analytics compare three groups of users:

| Cohort | What they receive |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Agentic messages. |
| **Legacy** | Template-based [emails](/features/emails) and [push notifications](/features/push-notifications), if you send them. |
| **Control** | No messages from Trophy. These are the users in your [control group](/platform/experimentation#control-ratio). |

Comparing the agent cohort with the control cohort shows the total impact of agentic messaging. Comparing it with the legacy cohort shows how it performs against the template-based messaging it replaces.

## Retention {#retention}

The retention chart shows the share of users in each cohort who are still active a set number of days after they were created, grouped by when the user was created.

## Impact {#impact}

The impact table shows how the agent cohort compares with the legacy and control cohorts on each measure:

- **Retention**: the share of users still active after a set number of days.
- **Streaks continued**: how often users keep their [streaks](/features/streaks) going.
- **Reactivated**: how often users come back after a few days without activity.
- **{Metric} per active user**: one row for each metric weighted in your [objectives](/agentic-messaging/objectives).
- **Emails per active user** and **Push notifications per active user**: how many messages users receive.
- **Unsubscribes per 1,000 emails**.

Each comparison is shown with a 95% confidence interval. Results are highlighted green or red only when the interval rules out no difference, so you can tell real lift from noise.

<Tip>
  Agentic messaging often sends fewer messages than template-based messaging
  while improving retention. Watch the messages per active user rows alongside
  your objectives.
</Tip>

## Message engagement {#message-engagement}

The message engagement table breaks down performance by channel, and by whether messages were sent by agents or by legacy templates:

- **Open rate**: email opens, or push notifications that opened your app.
- **Click rate**: messages where the user clicked a link.
- **Reengage rate**: messages after which the user came back to your app.
- **👍 rate** and **👎 rate**: how users rated your emails.

### Message ratings {#message-ratings}

Users can rate each agentic email with a 👍 or 👎. Ratings tell you how users feel about the messages they receive, and agents learn from them too, sending more of what users like and less of what they don't.

## Viewing messages {#viewing-messages}

The [Queue](https://app.trophy.so/agentic-messaging/queue) page lists agentic messages in two tabs:

- **Scheduled**: messages agents plan to send, with the user, channel and send time.
- **Sent**: messages that have already been sent.

Click any message to preview it. Previews are filled in with today's data, so values may differ from what was or will be sent.

You can also see the agentic messages scheduled for and sent to a specific user on their profile in the [Users](https://app.trophy.so/users) page.
