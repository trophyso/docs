Trophy is the 1:1 customer engagement platform for consumer web and mobile apps. Instead of moving every user through the same generic sequences, Trophy deploys an AI lifecycle agent for each user. The agent delivers the right message at the right time, alongside in-app features like milestones, streaks, points and leaderboards that give users a reason to come back.

## Agentic messaging {#agentic-messaging}

Trophy's AI lifecycle agents read each user's in-app activity and pick the best message for that user at the best moment. They write each email and push notification in your brand voice and stay inside the guardrails you set. Sometimes the best message is no message at all.

Agentic messaging is set up together with the Trophy team. [Book a call](https://trophy.so/contact-sales?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=introduction) to get started.

## Pricing {#pricing}

Trophy charges by **monthly active users (MAUs)**, so you never pay for churn. An MAU is a user who sends at least one [metric event](/data-sources/events) in a given calendar month.

There are three plans: Pro, Business and Enterprise. AI lifecycle agents and every in-app feature are included on all plans. [Book a demo](https://trophy.so/contact-sales?utm_source=docs) and we'll help you find the right plan for your team.
