---
title: Points changed
openapi: "/openapi/application.json webhook points.changed"
---

<Note>
  Points changed webhooks fire maximum once every 15 minutes per user.
</Note>

<Note>
  Points granted by redeeming a [reward](/features/rewards) also fire this webhook, but only once the reward is redeemed. Awarding a reward on its own does not change a user's points.
</Note>
