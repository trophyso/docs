---
title: Reward awarded
openapi: "/openapi/application.json webhook reward.awarded"
---

<Note>
  Awarding a reward does not apply it. Points rewards only add points, and
  custom rewards are only fulfilled, once the reward is redeemed. Use the
  [reward redeemed](/webhooks/events/rewards/reward-redeemed) webhook to know
  when that happens.
</Note>
